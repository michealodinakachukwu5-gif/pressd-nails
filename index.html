Questions, answered.

How long do Press’d Nails last?

With the included glue tabs, 1 to 2 weeks of wear. With nail glue, 2 to 4 weeks. Every set is reusable, so you can remove, clean and re-wear it many times.

Will they damage my natural nails?

No. Glue tabs sit on the surface, and removal is gentle with warm water and oil, so your natural nails stay healthy underneath.

How do I know my size?

Measure the widest point of each nail and match it to the size guide in the order form. Not sure? Send us a photo of your hand on WhatsApp and we’ll size you.

How long does an order take?

Ready-to-ship sets go out within 24 hours. Signature sets take about 48 hours. Bespoke sets take 48 hours to 5 days depending on the design. Delivery in Abuja is same-day or next-day.

Do you deliver outside Abuja?

Yes, nationwide across Nigeria. Delivery within Abuja is free on orders over ₦20,000. For other locations we confirm the fee on WhatsApp before you pay.

Can I order for a group or event?

Yes. We do bridal parties, owambe squads and corporate gifting with bulk pricing and matching designs. Choose “Bridal / event” in the order form or message us for a quote.
